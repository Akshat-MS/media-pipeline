Gap 1 — Monospace cannot carry subscripts
Your decks use Pi, Rj, and set notation like P = {P1, P2, … Pn}. Monospace forces every glyph to the same width, so subscripts cannot shrink or sit below the baseline — they render as full-size characters on the line.
